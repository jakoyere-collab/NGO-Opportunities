# Finding LinkedIn Postings to Manually Share (Not Automated)

This is a manual workflow, not part of `fetch_opportunities.py`. LinkedIn's Terms of Service
prohibit scraping or automated collection of posts — including of your own logged-in feed —
so this can't be turned into a script the way ReliefWeb/BambooHR/etc. were. Use this as a
checklist for finding and vetting individual "we're hiring" posts (not the official LinkedIn
Jobs board) to share in the WhatsApp group yourself.

## 1. Search queries to run yourself in LinkedIn

Run these in the main LinkedIn search bar, then click the **Posts** tab (not Jobs/People) to
search post content instead of the structured jobs board. Sort by **"Latest"** and use the
**Date posted** filter (Past 24 hours / Past week) to keep results current.

General hiring-post queries:
```
("we are hiring" OR "now hiring" OR "urgently recruiting" OR "vacancy" OR "job opening") AND (NGO OR nonprofit OR "non-profit" OR "international development" OR humanitarian) AND (Nigeria OR Nigerian OR Lagos OR Abuja OR "West Africa")
```
```
("send your CV" OR "apply here" OR "how to apply") AND NGO AND Nigeria
```

Role-specific (swap in whatever roles your group asks about most):
```
("Program Officer" OR "M&E Officer" OR "Project Coordinator" OR "Field Officer") AND NGO AND (Nigeria OR hiring)
```

Tips:
- Quotes = exact phrase, `OR`/`AND` = boolean, parentheses = grouping — LinkedIn search supports all three.
- Searching while logged in from a Nigeria-based or Nigeria-connected account tends to surface more locally-relevant posts.
- Repeat the search every few days rather than once — LinkedIn's post search only covers what's indexed recently, and hiring posts scroll out of relevance fast.

## 2. Hashtags and pages to check directly

Hashtag pages (search the hashtag, then click "Follow" so new posts show in your feed):
- `#NGOjobs` `#NGOjobsNigeria` `#HumanitarianJobs` `#DevelopmentJobs`
- `#JobsInNigeria` `#NigeriaJobs` `#NonprofitJobs` `#NowHiring`

Also worth following directly:
- HR/recruitment staff at INGOs active in Nigeria (UNICEF Nigeria, WFP Nigeria, Save the Children Nigeria, Mercy Corps Nigeria, IRC Nigeria, etc.) — they often post openings personally before or alongside the official board.
- Any Nigeria-focused NGO/development LinkedIn Groups you're already a member of.

## 3. Vetting checklist before posting to the WhatsApp group

Since these are individual posts, not the pipeline's certificate-checked, official-source
listings, treat each one like you would any unsolicited job lead:

- [ ] **Poster looks real** — real name, photo, work history consistent with the claim (e.g. they actually work in HR/recruitment at that org, not an unrelated role or an empty profile)
- [ ] **Organization is verifiable** — has a real, findable website; the post isn't the only place this "opening" exists
- [ ] **Cross-check on the org's own site** — if their careers page lists the same role, that's strong confirmation; if it doesn't exist there at all, be more cautious
- [ ] **Application method is legitimate** — link goes to the org's own domain or official email address, not a personal Gmail/Yahoo address, a WhatsApp number, or a generic third-party form
- [ ] **No payment or fee requested** — any request for money upfront (registration, "processing fee," training fee) is a scam, full stop
- [ ] **No request for sensitive info** — a real employer doesn't need your bank details or ID numbers at the application stage
- [ ] **Reasonably recent** — posted within the last several days, consistent with how fast the site's own listings turn over (10-day window)

If a posting fails any of the red-flag checks (payment, sensitive info, no verifiable org), don't share it — flag it as a scam instead if it seems worth warning the group about.

## 4. WhatsApp post template

Use this so manually-found postings look consistent with everything else shared in the group,
and so members know it's less vetted than the automated listings on the site:

```
🆕 *Job Alert* (via LinkedIn)
*[Job Title]* at *[Organization]*
📍 [Location / Remote]
🗓️ Posted: [date]
👉 Apply: [link]

⚠️ Found via an individual's LinkedIn post, not the org's official careers page — please verify the organization and link before applying.
```
